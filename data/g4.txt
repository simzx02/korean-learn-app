-(으)ㄴ/는 셈이다|"it amounts to, it is practically…"|Verb present: stem + 는 셈이다; verb past and adjective: stem + (으)ㄴ 셈이다|Says that, all things considered, the situation is practically the same as something else, even if not literally so.|열 명 중에서 아홉 명이 왔으니까 거의 다 온 셈이다.=Nine out of ten people came, so practically everyone came.|하루에 한 끼만 먹으니까 거의 굶는 셈이에요.=I only eat one meal a day, so I'm practically starving.
-(으)ㄹ 뻔하다|"almost, nearly (did)"|Verb stem ending in a consonant + 을 뻔하다; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄹ 뻔하다; always used in the past: 뻔했다|Something nearly happened but did not, usually something undesirable.|길이 미끄러워서 넘어질 뻔했어요.=The road was slippery and I almost fell.|늦잠을 자서 비행기를 놓칠 뻔했다.=I overslept and nearly missed my flight.
-(으)ㄴ/는 탓에|"because of (blame)" — negative cause|Verb present: stem + 는 탓에; verb past and adjective: stem + (으)ㄴ 탓에; noun + 탓에|Attributes a bad result to a cause. The opposite in tone of -(으)ㄴ/는 덕분에.|어제 과식한 탓에 배탈이 났다.=I got a stomachache because I overate yesterday.|성격이 급한 탓에 실수를 자주 한다.=Because I'm impatient by nature, I often make mistakes.
-(으)ㄴ/는 덕분에|"thanks to"|Verb present: stem + 는 덕분에; verb past: stem + (으)ㄴ 덕분에; noun + 덕분에|Attributes a good result to a cause, with a feeling of gratitude.|선생님께서 도와주신 덕분에 시험에 합격했습니다.=Thanks to my teacher's help, I passed the exam.|친구들 덕분에 한국 생활에 빨리 적응했다.=Thanks to my friends, I adapted quickly to life in Korea.
-(으)ㄴ/는 반면에|"on the other hand, whereas"|Verb present: stem + 는 반면에; verb past and adjective: stem + (으)ㄴ 반면에|Contrasts two opposing facts about the same or different subjects. Common in TOPIK II writing when describing graphs.|이 집은 교통이 편리한 반면에 월세가 비싸다.=This house has convenient transportation, but on the other hand the rent is expensive.|수출은 증가한 반면에 수입은 감소했다.=Exports increased, whereas imports decreased.
-(으)ㄴ/는 대신에|"instead of; in exchange for"|Verb stem + 는 대신에; adjective stem + (으)ㄴ 대신에; noun + 대신에|Either one action replaces another, or one thing compensates for another.|주말에 일하는 대신에 월요일에 쉬기로 했다.=I decided to take Monday off in exchange for working on the weekend.|밥 대신에 빵을 먹었어요.=I ate bread instead of rice.
-더라도|"even if"|Stem + 더라도; past: -았/었더라도|Concedes a hypothetical or real condition and says the main clause holds regardless. Stronger and more hypothetical than -아도/어도.|아무리 바쁘더라도 식사는 꼭 하세요.=No matter how busy you are, be sure to eat.|실패하더라도 다시 도전할 것이다.=Even if I fail, I will try again.
-(으)ㄹ 리가 없다|"there's no way that…"|Stem ending in a consonant + 을 리가 없다; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄹ 리가 없다; past: -았/었을 리가 없다|Expresses the speaker's strong belief that something cannot be true.|그렇게 성실한 사람이 거짓말을 할 리가 없어요.=There's no way such a sincere person would lie.|벌써 다 끝났을 리가 없다.=It can't possibly be finished already.
-(으)ㄴ/는 김에|"while you're at it; since you're … anyway"|Verb present: stem + 는 김에; verb past: stem + (으)ㄴ 김에|Takes the opportunity of one action to do another, unplanned one.|시장에 가는 김에 과일 좀 사다 주세요.=Since you're going to the market anyway, please buy some fruit for me.|서울에 온 김에 친구도 만나고 가려고 해요.=Since I've come to Seoul, I'm planning to see a friend before I leave.
-(으)ㄹ 만하다|"be worth doing"; "be doable / bearable"|Verb stem ending in a consonant + 을 만하다; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄹ 만하다|Says something has enough value to be done, or is at a level one can manage.|그 영화는 한번 볼 만해요.=That movie is worth watching once.|조금 맵지만 먹을 만합니다.=It's a little spicy, but it's edible enough.
-는 한|"as long as"|Verb stem + 는 한|States a condition that must continue to hold for the main clause to be true.|포기하지 않는 한 기회는 다시 올 것이다.=As long as you don't give up, an opportunity will come again.|내가 살아 있는 한 그 일을 잊지 않겠다.=As long as I live, I will not forget that.
-기 마련이다|"it is natural that…, bound to"|Stem + 기 마련이다|States something as a general truth or the natural course of things. Similar to -는 법이다.|누구나 실수를 하기 마련이다.=Everyone is bound to make mistakes.|시간이 지나면 잊히기 마련이에요.=As time passes, things are naturally forgotten.
-(으)ㄴ 채(로)|"while remaining in the state of…"|Verb stem ending in a consonant + 은 채로; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄴ 채로|The second action happens while the state resulting from the first action continues.|어제 불을 켠 채로 잠이 들었다.=Last night I fell asleep with the light on.|신발을 신은 채로 방에 들어가면 안 돼요.=You mustn't go into the room with your shoes on.
-아/어 봤자|"even if you try…, it's no use"|Stem with ㅏ or ㅗ + 아 봤자; other stems + 어 봤자|The speaker judges that the action would be useless, or that something is not as much as claimed.|지금 가 봤자 이미 문을 닫았을 거예요.=Even if you go now, it will already have closed.|아무리 말해 봤자 듣지 않을 것이다.=No matter how much you tell him, he won't listen.
-(으)ㄹ까 봐(서)|"for fear that, worried that… might"|Stem ending in a consonant + 을까 봐; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄹ까 봐; past: -았/었을까 봐|The speaker acts (or worries) because of a possible undesirable outcome.|시험에 떨어질까 봐 걱정이에요.=I'm worried that I might fail the exam.|늦을까 봐 택시를 타고 왔어요.=I took a taxi for fear of being late.
