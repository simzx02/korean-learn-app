-(으)ㄹ망정|"even though / even if… (still)"|Stem ending in a consonant + 을망정; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄹ망정|Concedes an unfavorable fact or extreme hypothesis, then asserts something that holds anyway. Literary.|가난하게 살망정 남에게 피해를 주고 싶지는 않다.=Even if I live in poverty, I don't want to do harm to others.|나이는 어릴망정 생각은 어른스럽다.=Young as he is, his thinking is mature.
-(으)ㄹ지언정|"I would rather… than; even if…"|Stem ending in a consonant + 을지언정; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄹ지언정|Accepts an extreme first option in order to firmly reject the second.|굶어 죽을지언정 그런 부정한 돈은 받지 않겠다.=I would rather starve to death than accept such dishonest money.|비난을 받을지언정 진실을 말해야 한다.=Even if you are criticized, you must tell the truth.
-건만|"although, even though" (contrary to expectation)|Stem + 건만; past: -았/었건만|The result is contrary to what the first clause would lead one to expect. Carries regret or frustration. Literary equivalent of -지만.|여러 번 설명했건만 아직도 이해하지 못했다.=Although I explained several times, he still hasn't understood.|봄이 왔건만 날씨는 여전히 쌀쌀하다.=Spring has come, yet the weather is still chilly.
-(으)련만|"it would be nice if…, but (it isn't so)"|Stem ending in a consonant + 으련만; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + 련만|Expresses a wish or expectation that reality fails to meet. Literary.|비라도 오면 좋으련만 하늘은 맑기만 하다.=It would be nice if it at least rained, but the sky is only clear.|조금만 더 노력하면 성공하련만 그는 쉽게 포기해 버렸다.=He would succeed with just a bit more effort, but he gave up easily.
-기 나름이다|"it depends on how one…"|Verb stem + 기 나름이다; noun + 나름이다|The outcome is determined by the way the action is done.|행복은 생각하기 나름이다.=Happiness depends on how you think about it.|아이의 성격은 부모가 가르치기 나름이에요.=A child's character depends on how the parents teach.
-(으)ㄹ 법하다|"it is plausible that, it might well…"|Stem ending in a consonant + 을 법하다; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄹ 법하다|The speaker judges that a situation is likely or reasonable enough to occur.|그 사람이라면 그런 말을 할 법하다.=If it's him, he might well say such a thing.|소설에나 나올 법한 일이 실제로 일어났다.=Something you would expect to find only in a novel actually happened.
-는 바 / -(으)ㄴ 바|"that which; the fact that" (formal nominalizer)|Verb present: stem + 는 바; verb past: stem + (으)ㄴ 바|A formal bound noun meaning "the thing / content that…". Common in set phrases: 아는 바, 느낀 바, 시사하는 바가 크다. As a connector, -(으)ㄴ 바 can also mean "having done…, (it was found that)".|이번 경험을 통해 느낀 바가 많다.=There is much that I felt through this experience.|위에서 언급한 바와 같이 원인은 복합적이다.=As mentioned above, the causes are complex.
-노라면|"if one keeps doing…, (eventually)"|Verb stem + 노라면|Says that continuing an action will naturally lead to a result. Literary equivalent of -다 보면.|열심히 살아가노라면 좋은 날도 올 것이다.=If you keep living diligently, good days will come too.|이 길을 따라 걷노라면 작은 호수가 나온다.=If you keep walking along this path, you come to a small lake.
-기에 망정이지|"luckily…, otherwise…"|Stem + 기에 망정이지; past: -았/었기에 망정이지|A fortunate circumstance prevented a bad outcome, which is then stated with -(으)ㄹ 뻔했다 or -았/었을 것이다.|일찍 출발했기에 망정이지 비행기를 놓칠 뻔했다.=Luckily we left early; otherwise we would have missed the plane.|우산을 가져왔기에 망정이지 비를 다 맞을 뻔했어요.=Good thing I brought an umbrella, or I would have been soaked.
-(으)ㄹ세라|"for fear that, lest"|Stem ending in a consonant + 을세라; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄹ세라|The subject acts carefully out of worry that something might happen. Literary.|아기가 깰세라 조용히 문을 닫았다.=She closed the door quietly lest the baby wake.|누가 들을세라 작은 목소리로 속삭였다.=He whispered in a small voice for fear that someone might hear.
-는 둥 마는 둥|"half-heartedly; barely doing"|Verb stem + 는 둥 마는 둥 (하다)|An action is done so carelessly that it is hard to say whether it was done at all.|늦잠을 자서 아침을 먹는 둥 마는 둥 하고 집을 나왔다.=I overslept, so I barely touched my breakfast before leaving the house.|그는 내 말을 듣는 둥 마는 둥 휴대폰만 보고 있었다.=He was only looking at his phone, half-listening to what I said.
-(으)ㄴ들|"even if / granted that… (it would make no difference)"|Stem ending in a consonant + 은들; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄴ들; noun + 인들|Concedes a condition and then, usually with a rhetorical question, says the outcome would not change.|지금 후회한들 무슨 소용이 있겠는가?=What use would it be even if you regret it now?|아무리 돈이 많은들 건강을 잃으면 아무 의미가 없다.=However much money you have, it means nothing if you lose your health.
(으)로 말미암아|"owing to, as a result of"|Noun ending in a consonant (except ㄹ) + 으로 말미암아; noun ending in a vowel or ㄹ + 로 말미암아|States a cause in formal written language. Similar to (으)로 인해.|전쟁으로 말미암아 수많은 사람이 고향을 잃었다.=Owing to the war, countless people lost their hometowns.|작은 오해로 말미암아 두 사람의 관계가 멀어졌다.=As a result of a small misunderstanding, the two grew apart.
을/를 막론하고|"regardless of"|Noun + 을/를 막론하고|From 莫論 ("not to be discussed"). Says that the stated distinction makes no difference. Common with paired opposites: 남녀노소, 동서고금, 이유 여하.|이 노래는 남녀노소를 막론하고 누구나 좋아한다.=Everyone likes this song, regardless of age or gender.|이유 여하를 막론하고 폭력은 용납될 수 없다.=Whatever the reason, violence cannot be tolerated.
을/를 무릅쓰고|"in spite of, braving (a risk or difficulty)"|Noun + 을/를 무릅쓰고|The subject pushes ahead while accepting a danger, opposition or hardship. Typical nouns: 위험, 반대, 추위, 실례.|소방관들은 위험을 무릅쓰고 불 속으로 뛰어들었다.=The firefighters braved the danger and rushed into the flames.|부모님의 반대를 무릅쓰고 유학을 떠났다.=I left to study abroad in spite of my parents' opposition.
