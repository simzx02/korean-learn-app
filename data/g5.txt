-(으)ㄹ지라도|"even though, even if" (emphatic, formal)|Stem ending in a consonant + 을지라도; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄹ지라도; past: -았/었을지라도|A strong concession used in writing and formal speech. The main clause holds regardless of the conceded condition.|비록 실패할지라도 도전해 볼 가치가 있다.=Even if it fails, it is worth attempting.|결과가 나쁠지라도 최선을 다했다면 후회는 없다.=Even if the result is bad, there is no regret if you did your best.
-는 한편|"while also; at the same time"|Verb stem + 는 한편|The subject carries out two things in parallel. Common in news and reports.|정부는 규제를 완화하는 한편 안전 관리는 강화하기로 했다.=The government decided to ease regulations while at the same time strengthening safety management.|그는 회사에 다니는 한편 밤에는 대학원에서 공부한다.=He works at a company and, alongside that, studies at graduate school at night.
-(으)ㄴ/는 가운데|"in the midst of, while"|Verb present: stem + 는 가운데; verb past and adjective: stem + (으)ㄴ 가운데|Describes the background situation in which the main event takes place. Typical of news style.|많은 시민이 지켜보는 가운데 개막식이 열렸다.=The opening ceremony was held with many citizens watching.|바쁘신 가운데 참석해 주셔서 감사합니다.=Thank you for attending despite your busy schedule.
-(으)ㄹ 따름이다|"only, nothing but"|Stem ending in a consonant + 을 따름이다; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄹ 따름이다|A formal equivalent of -(으)ㄹ 뿐이다: there is nothing beyond what is stated.|도와주신 모든 분께 그저 감사할 따름입니다.=I am simply grateful to everyone who helped.|저는 맡은 일을 했을 따름입니다.=I merely did the work I was assigned.
-기 십상이다|"be very likely to, be apt to" (negative outcome)|Verb stem + 기 십상이다|From 十常 ("eight or nine out of ten"). Used to warn that a bad result is highly likely.|준비 없이 사업을 시작하면 실패하기 십상이다.=If you start a business without preparation, you are very likely to fail.|서두르다 보면 실수하기 십상이다.=If you rush, you are apt to make mistakes.
-(으)ㄴ/는 만큼|"as much as"; "since, given that"|Verb present: stem + 는 만큼; verb past and adjective: stem + (으)ㄴ 만큼; noun + 만큼|Either the main clause is proportional to the first clause, or the first clause is the grounds for the second.|노력한 만큼 좋은 결과가 있을 것이다.=You will get results in proportion to the effort you made.|기대가 큰 만큼 실망도 컸다.=As my expectations were high, my disappointment was great too.
-다시피|"as you (know, see, hear)"; -다시피 하다: "practically, almost"|Verb stem + 다시피 (mainly with 알다, 보다, 듣다, 느끼다); verb stem + 다시피 하다|First use confirms something the listener already perceives. Second use says an action is done to an almost literal degree.|아시다시피 요즘 경기가 좋지 않습니다.=As you know, the economy is not good these days.|시험 기간에는 도서관에서 살다시피 했다.=During exams I practically lived in the library.
-(으)ㄴ 나머지|"as a result of being so…" (excess)|Stem ending in a consonant + 은 나머지; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄴ 나머지|An excessive state or action leads to a result, usually a negative or unintended one.|너무 긴장한 나머지 준비한 말을 다 잊어버렸다.=I was so nervous that I forgot everything I had prepared to say.|기쁜 나머지 눈물을 흘렸다.=She was so happy that she shed tears.
-는 통에|"because of (the commotion of)…"|Verb stem + 는 통에; noun + 통에|A chaotic or hectic situation causes a negative result.|아이들이 떠드는 통에 전화 소리를 듣지 못했다.=I couldn't hear the phone because the children were making such a racket.|사람들이 한꺼번에 몰려드는 통에 정신이 없었다.=With people crowding in all at once, I was overwhelmed.
-(으)ㄹ 바에야|"if (I) have to… anyway, I'd rather…"|Verb stem ending in a consonant + 을 바에야; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄹ 바에야|The first option is unsatisfactory, so the second is preferable. Often followed by 차라리.|이렇게 불편하게 살 바에야 차라리 이사를 가겠다.=If I have to live this uncomfortably, I'd rather move.|마음에 안 드는 옷을 살 바에야 안 사는 것이 낫다.=Rather than buy clothes you don't like, it's better not to buy any.
-거니와|"not only… but moreover"|Stem + 거니와; past: -았/었거니와|Acknowledges one fact and adds a further one in the same direction. Formal and written.|그 식당은 음식 맛도 좋거니와 서비스도 훌륭하다.=That restaurant's food is good, and moreover the service is excellent.|시간도 없거니와 돈도 부족하다.=Not only is there no time, but money is short as well.
-(으)므로|"because, therefore" (formal, written)|Stem ending in a consonant + 으므로; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + 므로; past: -았/었으므로|States a logical reason. Found in essays, official notices and speeches.|이 지역은 위험하므로 출입을 금지합니다.=This area is dangerous; therefore entry is prohibited.|위 학생은 성적이 우수하므로 이 상을 수여합니다.=This award is given because the above student's grades are excellent.
-고자|"in order to, intending to" (formal)|Verb stem + 고자; -고자 하다: "intend to"|A formal equivalent of -(으)려고. Frequent in presentations, speeches and the introductions of essays.|이 글에서는 저출산의 원인을 살펴보고자 한다.=In this essay, I intend to examine the causes of the low birth rate.|여러분의 의견을 듣고자 이 자리를 마련했습니다.=We arranged this occasion in order to hear your opinions.
은/는커녕|"far from…, let alone…"|Noun + 은/는커녕; verb or adjective stem + 기는커녕|Denies the first item emphatically, then states that even a lesser thing is not the case, or that the opposite is true.|저축은커녕 생활비도 부족하다.=Far from saving, I don't even have enough for living expenses.|칭찬을 듣기는커녕 오히려 야단을 맞았다.=Far from being praised, I got scolded instead.
-더니|"(I saw/heard that)… and then / but now…"|Stem + 더니 (subject is usually 2nd or 3rd person); -았/었더니 (subject is usually the speaker)|Links something the speaker directly observed to a later result, change or contrast.|동생이 열심히 공부하더니 장학금을 받았다.=My younger sibling studied hard, and then got a scholarship.|약을 먹었더니 금방 나았어요.=I took the medicine, and I recovered right away.
