-(으)면|"if; when(ever)"|Stem ending in a consonant + 으면; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + 면|States a condition or a general "whenever" situation.|시간이 있으면 같이 영화를 봐요.=If you have time, let's watch a movie together.|봄이 되면 꽃이 펴요.=When spring comes, flowers bloom.
-(으)니까|"because, since"; also "when I did… (I found)"|Stem ending in a consonant + 으니까; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + 니까 (ㄹ drops)|Gives a reason, often the speaker's own judgment. Unlike -아서/어서, it can take past tense and can be followed by commands and suggestions.|비가 오니까 우산을 가져가세요.=It's raining, so take an umbrella.|시간이 없으니까 택시를 탑시다.=We don't have time, so let's take a taxi.
-기 때문에|"because"|Stem + 기 때문에; noun + 때문에; past: -았/었기 때문에|A clear, somewhat formal statement of cause. Not followed by commands or suggestions.|눈이 많이 오기 때문에 길이 미끄러워요.=The road is slippery because it's snowing a lot.|감기 때문에 학교에 못 갔어요.=I couldn't go to school because of a cold.
-(으)려고|"in order to, intending to"|Verb stem ending in a consonant + 으려고; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + 려고|Expresses the purpose or intention behind the action in the main clause. -(으)려고 하다 means "to plan to".|한국 회사에 취직하려고 한국어를 배워요.=I'm learning Korean in order to get a job at a Korean company.|주말에 등산을 하려고 해요.=I'm planning to go hiking on the weekend.
-(으)러 가다/오다|"go / come in order to"|Verb stem ending in a consonant + 으러; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + 러, followed by a movement verb|Expresses the purpose of going or coming. Only used with movement verbs such as 가다, 오다, 다니다.|점심을 먹으러 식당에 가요.=I'm going to the restaurant to eat lunch.|친구가 저를 만나러 한국에 왔어요.=My friend came to Korea to see me.
-아야/어야 하다|"must, have to"|Stem with ㅏ or ㅗ + 아야 하다; other stems + 어야 하다; 하다 → 해야 하다|Expresses obligation or necessity. 되다 can replace 하다 with the same meaning.|내일까지 숙제를 내야 해요.=I have to hand in my homework by tomorrow.|약을 먹은 후에 푹 쉬어야 돼요.=You must rest well after taking the medicine.
-(으)ㄹ 수 있다/없다|"can / cannot"|Stem ending in a consonant + 을 수 있다; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄹ 수 있다|Expresses ability or possibility.|저는 한국어로 이메일을 쓸 수 있어요.=I can write emails in Korean.|너무 매워서 먹을 수 없어요.=It's so spicy that I can't eat it.
-아도/어도 되다|"may, it's okay to"|Stem with ㅏ or ㅗ + 아도 되다; other stems + 어도 되다; 하다 → 해도 되다|Asks for or gives permission.|여기에서 사진을 찍어도 돼요?=May I take pictures here?|내일은 늦게 와도 돼요.=It's okay to come late tomorrow.
-(으)면 안 되다|"must not, shouldn't"|Stem ending in a consonant + 으면 안 되다; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + 면 안 되다|Expresses prohibition.|도서관에서 큰 소리로 이야기하면 안 돼요.=You must not talk loudly in the library.|여기에 주차하면 안 됩니다.=You may not park here.
-고 있다|"be doing" (action in progress)|Verb stem + 고 있다|Describes an action in progress. With verbs of wearing (입다, 쓰다, 신다) it describes the resulting state.|지금 저녁을 먹고 있어요.=I'm eating dinner now.|동생은 빨간 모자를 쓰고 있어요.=My younger sibling is wearing a red hat.
-아/어 주다|"do something for someone"|Stem with ㅏ or ㅗ + 아 주다; other stems + 어 주다; 하다 → 해 주다|Shows the action is done as a favor. -아/어 주세요 makes a polite request; use 드리다 instead of 주다 when the receiver is someone you should honor.|창문 좀 닫아 주세요.=Please close the window.|친구가 숙제를 도와줬어요.=My friend helped me with my homework.
-는 / -(으)ㄴ / -(으)ㄹ + noun|noun-modifying forms: "the person who…", "the food that…"|Verb present: stem + 는. Verb past: stem + (으)ㄴ. Future: stem + (으)ㄹ. Adjective: stem + (으)ㄴ. 있다/없다: + 는.|Turns a clause into a modifier placed before a noun.|저기에서 책을 읽는 사람이 제 동생이에요.=The person reading a book over there is my younger sibling.|어제 먹은 음식이 맛있었어요.=The food I ate yesterday was delicious.
-(으)ㄴ 적이 있다/없다|"have (never) done" (experience)|Verb stem ending in a consonant + 은 적이 있다; stem ending in a vowel or ㄹ + ㄴ 적이 있다|Talks about past experience. Often combined with -아/어 보다: -아/어 본 적이 있다.|제주도에 가 본 적이 있어요.=I have been to Jeju Island.|저는 김치를 만든 적이 없어요.=I have never made kimchi.
-는데 / -(으)ㄴ데|background information; soft contrast: "…and / but / so…"|Verb stem + 는데; adjective stem + (으)ㄴ데; noun + 인데; past: -았/었는데|Sets the scene for what follows: a contrast, a question, a request or a suggestion. One of the most frequent connectors in spoken Korean.|비가 오는데 우산이 없어요.=It's raining, but I don't have an umbrella.|이 옷이 좀 작은데 더 큰 사이즈 있어요?=These clothes are a bit small; do you have a bigger size?
-기 전에 / -(으)ㄴ 후에|"before doing" / "after doing"|Verb stem + 기 전에; verb stem + (으)ㄴ 후에 (or -(으)ㄴ 다음에)|Orders two actions in time. With nouns: noun + 전에 / 후에.|자기 전에 이를 닦아요.=I brush my teeth before going to bed.|수업이 끝난 후에 도서관에 갈 거예요.=I'll go to the library after class ends.
